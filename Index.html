Who has this problem

Who they are

Working-age outpatients in Irish public hospitals who get a postal appointment letter and have work, childcare or transport to juggle.

Who pays or decides

Hospital management and the outpatient administration department.

Who uses it every day

Patients (to confirm or change) and scheduling staff (to see freed slots and refill them).

"I got the letter weeks ago. Then work booked me in for training that morning. I rang for 25 minutes and gave up." (Invented patient quote.)
"We have empty slots at 11:00 and a long waiting list, and nobody tells us in time." (Invented staff quote.)

What any solution must do

  • Let a patient cancel or request a new date in under a minute, on a phone, without ringing the hospital.
  • Send reminders 7 days and 24 hours before each appointment.
  • Work at any hour, not only office hours.
  • Show staff a freed slot as soon as it is released.
  • Offer freed slots to waiting-list patients.
  • Leave phone and letter contact open for patients who cannot use it.

Today, hospitals mix posted letters, phone calls and text reminders. Patients often still have to phone to cancel or change a time, so slots are lost even when the patient would have attended another day.

What CarePoint is

A proposed appointment management portal: a phone-friendly website for patients and a simple dashboard for hospital staff.

  1. Patient is reminded

    Reminders are due 7 days and 24 hours before the appointment, worked out from the appointment date.

  2. Patient confirms, cancels or requests a new date

    One tap on a phone. A cancelled slot shows as available on the staff dashboard straight away; a reschedule request alerts administration.

  3. The slot is refilled

    Staff offer it to a waiting patient, or waiting patients pick it themselves. Once taken, the slot is filled.

Real text messages, hospital system integration and secure login are planned, not built. A real version would need privacy protections before any real patient data is used.

Built for real situations

Phone first, large tap targets, short text, readable for older patients and people less confident with technology.

How CarePoint would be paid for

Hospitals pay an annual subscription. Patients never pay.

Small hospital
€30,000 / year

Roughly up to 40,000 outpatient appointments a year. Reminders, patient self-service, staff dashboard.

Regional hospital
€60,000 / year

Roughly 40,000 to 150,000 appointments a year. Adds waiting-list refill and reporting.

Large or university hospital
from €100,000 / year

150,000+ appointments, several clinics or sites. Quoted after a pilot.

These prices are assumptions for this concept, not quotes. Text-message costs and one-off setup or integration work would be extra. Pricing would be tested in a pilot.

Illustrative example: a hospital with 40,000 outpatient appointments a year and a 13% miss rate has about 5,200 misses. If CarePoint helped refill one in five, that is about 1,040 slots. At roughly €128 per missed appointment (€67 million divided by 525,000, from the HSE figures above), that is about €130,000 of clinical capacity. All of this is assumption, not a result.
